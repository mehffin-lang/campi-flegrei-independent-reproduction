Counts and the actual build outcome are in `data/continuation/analysis_summary.json`, `downstream_summary.json`, `hybrid_summary.json`, `panel_status.json`, and `rebuild_status.json`. No publication panel is fully reproduced. The newer executed follow-up is recorded in `data/calibration_followup/summary.json`, `validation.json`, `selection_sensitivity.json`, `relocation/summary.json` and `rebuild_status.json`; see `docs/CALIBRATION_FOLLOWUP.md`.

- **S6, S7, S9–S13, main 2–4:** verified numerical 2008 Vp/Vs background grid, coordinates, version and transformation to the distributed grid; exact Calò–Tramelli (2018) layered/interpolated starting profile; independently reviewed calibration phases; physically acceptable and numerically stable VELEST calibration; validated neighboring-event differential times, both TomoDD runs and regional HypoDD SVD. The follow-up executes both alternative-profile calibration rounds and five catalog passes (238 numerical-filter passes), but continued iterations move some locations by kilometers and the stricter selection produces nonphysical layers. The two exploratory HypoDD LSQR rounds and quadrant SVD are not TomoDD/S7: three largest components return conditional estimates, while NE diverges. Reviewed phase/event identities and a stable calibration are required; precision agreement alone is insufficient. The prior grid and dependent pilot are quarantined (**unverified provenance**). Replacing these stages with authors' fitted outputs is prohibited (**prompt restriction**).
- **S1 and all multiyear results:** finish the largely pending waveform queue and inference; distinguish observed intervals from availability/requested dates. New half-hour checks sample calendar dates across the study period but are not continuous coverage (**unfinished processing**). Full raw retention is estimated near 1 TB from retrieved chunk sizes, greater than currently free local capacity; this estimate is not a remote-access failure.
- **S2:** resolved metadata for nine released identifiers (eight IV identifiers and the NAP HN/HG channel-code mismatch) and deployment/alias records reconciling 51 identifiers with 48 instruments. Two coincident-coordinate pairs alone do not reconcile membership. Public RAN metadata now cover all 17 candidate station names, including POZA's January 2025 closure; waveform access remains separate (**missing metadata/membership**).
- **RAN observations:** registered waveform-download access; registration and authentication pages are public but no credentials are available (**access problem**). Public IV/2I observations remain accessible. The temporary archive contains author-event-selected 60-second waveform windows; it cannot establish independent continuous detection coverage, and its event identifiers cannot seed detections (**selection limitation**).
- **S3A–D, S4B:** exact training/benchmark examples, independently eligible annotations, split membership, augmentation details and labeled benchmark execution. **S4A is partial:** original and retrained weights were run on the caption-defined CSOB swarm, but the exact published window and labels are unavailable. More picks do not establish improved accuracy. Released weights support inference only (**missing inputs**).
- **S8A–B and magnitude-dependent panels:** final accepted independent locations; full exposure and adequate amplitude-pair coverage; calibration uncertainty and sensitivity assessment. Measurements now include retrieved stations without associated picks, using theoretical P times. Current amplitude inversion and formal uncertainty are conditional pilot calculations. Routine Md is permitted as a stated calibration prior. Authors' final magnitudes cannot fill missing results (**prompt restriction**).
- **Main 4A–B:** reviewed P polarities and S/P ratios; accepted locations/model and response-corrected event waveforms; FK Green's functions and complete event-specific SKHASH/gCAP settings; independent solutions before Kagan-angle comparison (**missing inputs/processing**). Data S3 is not a substitute (**prompt restriction**).
- **S12A–H:** relocated sector selection, waveform and suspicious-depth review. Independently P-aligned CPOZ HHZ/HHN gathers now use the documented eight-second, >4 Hz display; they are not the sector validation (**unfinished processing**).
- **S14 all groups; S15A/B/D:** complete CAAM/COLB coverage, onset/coda review, validated clusters, horizontal amplitude ratios and family timing; correctly selected counterpart examples. Crossing spectra and separately saved station scores are unconfirmed measurements, not hybrid detections. Six template-crossing examples and selected station windows for all nine negative-mean-FI catalog candidates were visually reviewed. Persistent oscillations, impulsive packets and apparent earlier-event coda occur; no hybrid family or reviewed onset labels were confirmed (**unfinished processing**).
- **1A/1C/S5:** publication-era routine-catalog snapshot and any undocumented selection/version details. The archive availability query found no October-2025 snapshot; the CDX query timed out. The live October-2026 catalog has 12,503 located study events, 420 more than the written publication count; no filter was tuned to match it (**missing snapshot**). Figure 1A/S5 now match the original visual style where supported, with disclosed full-depth color ranges and no altered numerical catalog values. Figure 3 and 4A–B have no valid independent counterparts to restyle.
- **1B:** exact benchmark equivalence and datum-joining recipe; calendar gaps remain. Daily observations already cover 2020–2023 and through 20 March 2025. Historical source estimates/reconstruction are now labeled explicitly (**method specification**).
