\begin{abstract}
Earthquake locations, magnitudes and source mechanisms constrain interpretations of unrest at Campi Flegrei. Following the observational study of Tan et al. (2025), this investigation processes original seismic waveforms and historical displacement observations. Independent leveling and GNSS processing recovers part of the displacement history. A limited waveform sample yields nine provisional associated events, without calibrated or double-difference locations. The available results therefore do not establish the publication's fault geometry, magnitude distribution or focal mechanisms. No publication panel is fully independently reproduced. Figure 1B and supplementary S1, S2 and S15C are partial; the remaining panels are explicitly marked not reproduced. The presentation follows the publication's scientific sequence, followed by its supplementary sequence and a separate reproduction record.
\end{abstract}
