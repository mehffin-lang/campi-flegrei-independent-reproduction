\begin{abstract}
Displacement observations, labeled historical source estimates and an external routine earthquake catalog reconstruct the historical context of unrest at Campi Flegrei. Daily GNSS observations now extend through 20 March 2025. Independent waveform processing produces 1,023 provisional GaMMA associations from 134,483 picks in the latest checkpoint, but has not established the publication's relocated fault geometry, magnitude distribution or focal mechanisms. No publication panel is fully independently reproduced. \input{report/continuation_panel_summary.tex} The scientific narrative and reproduced supplement precede the input, implementation, validation and completion records.
\end{abstract}
