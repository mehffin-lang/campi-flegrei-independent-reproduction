\section*{Materials and Methods}
\section{Building the Campi Flegrei high-resolution earthquake catalog}
\paragraph{Data.} The plotted station intervals describe the expanded processed checkpoint; the current grid-derived calibration uses that expanded set. Earlier magnitude and diagnostic products retain their separate event identifiers. The station inventory and intervals provide partial counterparts to Figures S1 and S2. They do not establish complete network coverage over the study period.
\paragraph{Phase picking.} Inference with released trained weights produced 136,376 merged picks (20,022 P and 116,354 S) in the processed observations. Figure S4A now has a partial inference comparison on the station and swarm specified in its caption. The original and retrained models return \BenchmarkOriginalP{} P/\BenchmarkOriginalS{} S and \BenchmarkRetrainedP{} P/\BenchmarkRetrainedS{} S picks respectively in the selected 120 s interval. These counts do not establish accuracy without reference labels. The exact published window is unverified. Training examples S3 and the exact S4B noise example remain unreproduced.
\paragraph{Phase association.} GaMMA associated 7,416 picks into 1,024 provisional events using the explicitly labeled published alternative starting profile. These are candidates, not a validated multiyear catalog.
\paragraph{Initial earthquake location.} The newly authorized-grid VELEST trial uses the expanded independent associations. The final small-update gate is reached after documented layer merging, but full-path precision and regional-start dependence remain consequential; no location set is accepted. Figure S6 shows the provisional numerical result; detailed validation follows the supplement. Figure S5 is the separate external routine-catalog reconstruction, with 12,503 located study-period events.
\paragraph{Earthquake relocation.} Two fixed-grid TomoDD rounds and regional HypoDD SVD analyses were executed. Figures S6--S7 and S10--S13 now show supported provisional numerical results. No event satisfies S9\textquotesingle s more-than-100-correlated-phase filter. All locations retain unresolved calibration and phase-validation limits.
\paragraph{Local magnitude.} The relocated subset gives 67 provisional magnitudes from 884 amplitude equations. Figure S8 shows calibration matches and the conditional magnitude distribution. Routine duration magnitudes are external anchors, not independent measurements or validation.
\paragraph{Focal mechanism estimation.} AI visual inspection of 180 station-event records produced 29 usable first motions and 41 usable S/P measurements across eight own candidates; no human manual review is claimed. SKHASH returns no solutions under unchanged quality gates. Generic FK and a disclosed gCAP port return seven provisional double-couple fits in each of two frequency bands. Their poor waveform fits and large orientation changes prevent acceptance. Figure 4 shows these numerical trials with an explicit unvalidated label; the post-supplement record lists every candidate and rejection.

\section{Validation of earthquake locations}
\paragraph{Ring-shaped structures with confined depth distributions.} The provisional associations do not establish a ring structure. Internal travel-time consistency is preserved in the documentation, and is complemented by provisional regional uncertainty and common-receiver tests in Figures S7 and S12.
\paragraph{Depth of earthquakes in the central region.} Figure S12 now displays 122 P-aligned CPOZ component traces with explicit alternative geographic masks. Relocated depths and onsets remain provisional. Neither these gathers nor the small SVD subsets establish the publication\textquotesingle s depth limits.

\section{Template Matching to Detect Hybrid Events}
The search covered 78.773 hours of available common CAAM/COLB intervals and returned 823 unconfirmed threshold crossings. Figure S14 displays an explicitly unconfirmed highest-correlation subset with measured FI and horizontal-amplitude ratios; no family is declared a confirmed hybrid cluster.
\paragraph{Depth.} No independent hybrid-event location set is available for Table S2.
\paragraph{Frequency index.} Frequency indices were calculated for the retained three templates and the earlier four candidate windows. The expanded scan now has saved eight-second CAAM frequency-index and signal/noise measurements. These use template alignment, not reviewed onsets; coda discrimination and family confirmation remain incomplete. Figure S15C displays the corresponding third written-method template. The first two written-method family templates appear as clearly labeled S15A/B analogues; they are different events and do not reproduce those exact examples.
\paragraph{Relative magnitude.} The candidate displays give log horizontal-amplitude ratios within each template group. These are not called relative magnitudes because shared source paths and cluster membership have not been validated.
\paragraph{Temporal evolution of the hybrid events.} The limited exposure and unconfirmed detections do not support a three-year cluster history.
\paragraph{Further search for HB/LP events.} The provisional catalog now has station-averaged frequency-index screening on retrieved vertical channels. Selected observed windows for every negative-mean candidate in this checkpoint were visually inspected. They include persistent oscillations, impulsive packets and apparent contamination by earlier-event coda; no hybrid family was confirmed. This limited review does not supply validated onset labels. Missing observations still prevent a complete multiyear search.
