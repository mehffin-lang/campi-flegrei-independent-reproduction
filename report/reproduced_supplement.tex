\section*{Materials and Methods}
\section{Building the Campi Flegrei high-resolution earthquake catalog}
\paragraph{Data.} The frozen extension uses 1,327 eligible continuous files, 14,080.521 channel-hours and 26 station names. Known event-selected snippets are excluded. Figure S1 uses this coverage; S2 retains verified geometry and uncertain instrument membership. Neither establishes full study-period coverage.
\paragraph{Phase picking.} Inference with released weights gives 256,720 merged picks (42,089 P and 214,631 S) in the frozen extension. Figure S4A now has a partial inference comparison on the station and swarm specified in its caption. The original and retrained models return \BenchmarkOriginalP{} P/\BenchmarkOriginalS{} S and \BenchmarkRetrainedP{} P/\BenchmarkRetrainedS{} S picks respectively in the selected 120 s interval. These counts do not establish accuracy without reference labels. The exact published window is unverified. Training examples S3 and the exact S4B noise example remain unreproduced.
\paragraph{Phase association.} GaMMA associates 31,096 phases into 3,296 provisional events using the disclosed alternative profile. No author event solution or event-selected snippet enters detection.
\paragraph{Initial earthquake location.} The observationally fitted alternative profile is tested with a separated 2025 holdout and remains unaccepted. Five subsequent whole-cohort passes yield 1,414 events passing numerical filters, plotted provisionally in Figure S6. These are distinct from the earlier grid-initialized locations used by the preserved TomoDD checkpoint.
\paragraph{Earthquake relocation.} Two fixed-grid TomoDD rounds retain 1050 provisional events. Regional HypoDD SVD returns 352 conditional estimates. 0 events reach 100 correlated phases for S9. Model and phase-validation limitations remain.
\paragraph{Local magnitude.} 868 own provisional magnitudes from 167234 amplitude equations and 374 external duration-magnitude priors; 308 measured ML--Md pairs are displayed. Conditional Mc=-0.10, b=0.822 +/- 0.032, N=592. External Md is calibration, not validation. Discontinuous sampling prevents a population-completeness claim.
\paragraph{Focal mechanism estimation.} 12 unaccepted own FK/gCAP trial fits using newly available EIDA records and the preserved grid-initialized model and its explicitly linked candidate geometry. 99 AI-reviewed polarities, 108 AI-reviewed ratios; no human review. SKHASH returns 2 solutions. Low-band variance reduction 1.3--14.4\%; orientations change 45.0--95.0 degrees between frequency bands. Gray beachballs denote unvalidated trials; background is the preserved earlier independent catalog, never joined to candidates by integer ID. SKHASH returns 2 quality-D trials. No scientifically accepted mechanism.

\section{Validation of earthquake locations}
\paragraph{Ring-shaped structures with confined depth distributions.} The provisional associations do not establish a ring structure. Internal travel-time consistency is preserved in the documentation, and is complemented by provisional regional uncertainty and common-receiver tests in Figures S7 and S12.
\paragraph{Depth of earthquakes in the central region.} 1168 observed CPOZ component traces for 584 events, aligned on own P picks and filtered above 4 Hz. Explicit alternative sectors, no interpolation between traces. Onsets and absolute depths remain unvalidated.

\section{Template Matching to Detect Hybrid Events}
The search covered 140.656 common CAAM/COLB hours and yielded 1843 unconfirmed crossings. Twelve highest scores per template displayed from original waveforms with measured frequency indices and amplitude ratios. No confirmed cluster or accepted hybrid magnitude. AI inspection of 36 displayed crossings found 4 preceding-packet tails and 3 ongoing-activity windows; the other transients remain unclassified. No human review.

\paragraph{Depth.} No independent hybrid-event location set is available for Table S2.
\paragraph{Frequency index.} Frequency indices were calculated for the retained three templates and the earlier four candidate windows. The expanded scan now has saved eight-second CAAM frequency-index and signal/noise measurements. These use template alignment, not reviewed onsets; coda discrimination and family confirmation remain incomplete. Figure S15C displays the corresponding third written-method template. The first two written-method family templates appear as clearly labeled S15A/B analogues; they are different events and do not reproduce those exact examples.
\paragraph{Shallow waveform example.} Own waveform example selected from a provisional depth below 0.6 km near CAAM. It is not the published event, and neither extremely shallow depth, VT source type, nor west-Accademia membership is validated.
\paragraph{Relative magnitude.} The candidate displays give log horizontal-amplitude ratios within each template group. These are not called relative magnitudes because shared source paths and cluster membership have not been validated.
\paragraph{Temporal evolution of the hybrid events.} The limited exposure and unconfirmed detections do not support a three-year cluster history.
\paragraph{Further search for HB/LP events.} The new provisional catalog has 67530 independent station frequency-index measurements for 3270 events; 107 have negative station-mean FI. These are screening results, not confirmed hybrids. Of the 107 negative-mean candidates, 62 have no measured station above 5 dB signal/noise. AI inspection of the highest-SNR station for 10 candidates did not establish a hybrid source; current onsets and coda have not all been visually validated. Earlier saved visual reviews refer to their historical cohort and are not silently transferred to the new events.
