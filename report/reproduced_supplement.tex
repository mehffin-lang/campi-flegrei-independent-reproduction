\section*{Materials and Methods}
\section{Building the Campi Flegrei high-resolution earthquake catalog}
\paragraph{Data.} The plotted station intervals describe the expanded processed checkpoint; the current grid-derived calibration uses that expanded set. Earlier magnitude and diagnostic products retain their separate event identifiers. The station inventory and intervals provide partial counterparts to Figures S1 and S2. They do not establish complete network coverage over the study period.
\paragraph{Phase picking.} Inference with released trained weights produced \CurrentPicks{} merged picks (\CurrentPPicks{} P and \CurrentSPicks{} S) in the processed observations. Figure S4A now has a partial inference comparison on the station and swarm specified in its caption. The original and retrained models return \BenchmarkOriginalP{} P/\BenchmarkOriginalS{} S and \BenchmarkRetrainedP{} P/\BenchmarkRetrainedS{} S picks respectively in the selected 120 s interval. These counts do not establish accuracy without reference labels. The exact published window is unverified. Training examples S3 and the exact S4B noise example remain unreproduced.
\paragraph{Phase association.} GaMMA associated \CurrentAssigned{} picks into \CurrentEvents{} provisional events using the explicitly labeled published alternative starting profile. These are candidates, not a validated multiyear catalog.
\paragraph{Initial earthquake location.} The newly authorized-grid VELEST trial uses the expanded independent associations. Local convergence is achieved after documented layer merging, but full-path precision and regional-start dependence remain consequential; no location set is accepted. Numerical results and validation are reported after the supplement. Figure S5 is the separate external routine-catalog reconstruction, with 12,503 located study-period events.
\paragraph{Earthquake relocation.} Original waveforms supply new coherency-weighted differential times for the grid-derived filtered trial. The current fixed-grid TomoDD execution and validation record follows the supplement. No validated relocated catalog or specified regional SVD analysis exists, so Figures S7 and S9--S13 remain unreproduced.
\paragraph{Local magnitude.} The pilot measured \CurrentAmplitudes{} channel amplitudes; \CurrentSNR{} passed the 5 dB SNR threshold. It formed \CurrentMagnitudeEdges{} relative-amplitude equations and returned \CurrentMagnitudes{} provisional magnitude estimates. Figure S8 displays the partial pilot comparison and distribution. This does not establish the final relocated catalog's magnitude distribution or completeness.
\paragraph{Focal mechanism estimation.} SKHASH and gCAP inversions were not run. No independent mechanism is available for Figure 4.

\section{Validation of earthquake locations}
\paragraph{Ring-shaped structures with confined depth distributions.} The provisional associations do not establish a ring structure. Internal travel-time consistency is preserved in the documentation, but does not substitute for the regional uncertainty or common-receiver tests in Figures S7 and S12.
\paragraph{Depth of earthquakes in the central region.} There are now \CurrentCPOZ{} associated CPOZ P arrivals in the provisional sample. P-aligned CPOZ waveform gathers have been saved as a reproduction diagnostic. The relocated sector selections and manual checks on apparently deep central events remain unperformed. No conclusion about a lower depth limit follows from their absence.

\section{Template Matching to Detect Hybrid Events}
The search covered \CurrentHybridHours{} hours of available common CAAM/COLB intervals and returned \CurrentHybridCandidates{} unconfirmed threshold crossings. It did not produce independently confirmed families for Figure S14.
\paragraph{Depth.} No independent hybrid-event location set is available for Table S2.
\paragraph{Frequency index.} Frequency indices were calculated for the retained three templates and the earlier four candidate windows. The expanded scan now has saved eight-second CAAM frequency-index and signal/noise measurements. These use template alignment, not reviewed onsets; coda discrimination and family confirmation remain incomplete. Figure S15C displays the corresponding third written-method template. The first two templates are different examples from the published S15A/B panels and are not inserted in their place.
\paragraph{Relative magnitude.} Cluster-relative magnitudes were not assigned because cluster membership has not been established.
\paragraph{Temporal evolution of the hybrid events.} The limited exposure and unconfirmed detections do not support a three-year cluster history.
\paragraph{Further search for HB/LP events.} The provisional catalog now has station-averaged frequency-index screening on retrieved vertical channels. Selected observed windows for every negative-mean candidate in this checkpoint were visually inspected. They include persistent oscillations, impulsive packets and apparent contamination by earlier-event coda; no hybrid family was confirmed. This limited review does not supply validated onset labels. Missing observations still prevent a complete multiyear search.
